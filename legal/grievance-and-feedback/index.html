How to raise a concern

Feedback and grievances about the Foundation's work or this website are welcome. Write to gramyasarvodaya@gmail.com with "Grievance" in the subject line, or call +91 96790 70943.

Please include your name, a way to reach you, and what happened. We will acknowledge your message and tell you how it will be looked into.

Official registration details, governing-body information, statutory registrations, and applicable tax exemption details will be added after verification from the Foundation's official documents.

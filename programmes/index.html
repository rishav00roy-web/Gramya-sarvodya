Twelveinitiatives

for all

Promote sustainable agriculture and natural farming. Strengthen farmer institutions and collective enterprises. Empower women and rural youth through skills and entrepreneurship.

  1. ASustainable Agriculture & Natural FarmingHealthy Soil, Healthy Crops, Prosperous Farmers.
  2. BFarmer Producer Company (FPC)We encourage farmers to work collectively through Farmer Producer Companies and producer institutions.
  3. CFarm Field School (FFS)Learn. Observe. Practice. Succeed.
  4. DAgro TourismExperience Agriculture. Embrace Nature. Enrich Lives.
  5. EGreen EducationLearn Green. Live Green. Lead Green.
  6. FGramya Udyog – Rural IndustriesLocal Resources. Local Skills. Local Enterprises. Global Impact.
  7. GNatural Treatment & Wellness CentreBack to Nature. Back to Health. Back to Life.
  8. HCommunity Financial InclusionWe promote financial literacy, responsible savings habits, entrepreneurship awareness and access to appropriate financial services.
  9. IWomen's EmpowermentEmpowered Women. Strong Families. Prosperous Communities.
  10. JYouth Empowerment & Skill DevelopmentWe work towards creating opportunities for rural youth through education, skills and entrepreneurship.
  11. KEnvironmental Conservation & Renewable EnergyWe promote responsible use of natural resources and environmentally sustainable development.
  12. LWaste-to-Wealth & Circular EconomyReduce. Reuse. Recycle. Regenerate.

How we work, step by step

Our approach combines local knowledge, scientific understanding and collective action.

  1. 1Community identification and participation.
  2. 2Assessment of local needs and resources.
  3. 3Capacity building and skill development.
  4. 4Institutional development.
  5. 5Livelihood and enterprise creation.
  6. 6Market and technology linkages.
  7. 7Monitoring and evaluation.
  8. 8Long-term community sustainability.

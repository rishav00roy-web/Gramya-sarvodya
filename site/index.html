Welfare of all

Sarvodaya

Inspired by Gandhian philosophy, Gramya Sarvodaya Foundation works towards creating self-reliant villages through sustainable agriculture, rural entrepreneurship, education, environmental conservation, community empowerment and inclusive development.

Know moreJoin our movement

We believe that the real progress of a nation begins with the development of its villages. Our approach combines traditional knowledge, local resources, community participation and modern innovation to build resilient and prosperous rural communities.

Our philosophy

Inspired by Gandhian ideology

Our name represents two powerful ideas:

Gramya
Village, rural life and community-based development.
Sarvodaya
The welfare, upliftment and prosperity of all.

Together, Gramya Sarvodaya represents the holistic development of rural communities for the welfare of society.

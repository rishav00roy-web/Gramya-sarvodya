Twelveinitiatives

Promote sustainable agriculture and natural farming. Strengthen farmer institutions and collective enterprises. Empower women and rural youth through skills and entrepreneurship.

These pages set out the work the Foundation intends to do in each area. Projects will be listed on the Our Impact page as they begin, with what they have actually done.

  1. Sustainable Agriculture & Natural FarmingHealthy Soil, Healthy Crops, Prosperous Farmers.
  2. Farmer Producer Company (FPC) SupportWe encourage farmers to work collectively through Farmer Producer Companies and producer institutions.
  3. Farm Field School (FFS)Learn. Observe. Practice. Succeed.
  4. Agro TourismExperience Agriculture. Embrace Nature. Enrich Lives.
  5. Green EducationLearn Green. Live Green. Lead Green.
  6. Gramya Udyog – Rural IndustriesLocal Resources. Local Skills. Local Enterprises. Global Impact.
  7. Natural Wellness InitiativeBack to Nature. Back to Health. Back to Life.
  8. Community Financial InclusionWe promote financial literacy, responsible savings habits, entrepreneurship awareness and access to appropriate financial services.
  9. Women's EmpowermentEmpowered Women. Strong Families. Prosperous Communities.
  10. Youth Empowerment & Skill DevelopmentWe work towards creating opportunities for rural youth through education, skills and entrepreneurship.
  11. Environmental Conservation & Renewable EnergyWe promote responsible use of natural resources and environmentally sustainable development.
  12. Waste-to-Wealth & Circular EconomyReduce. Reuse. Recycle. Regenerate.

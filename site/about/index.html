Whowe are

Gramya Sarvodaya Foundation is a rural development-oriented organization dedicated to promoting inclusive, sustainable and community-led development.

Community-led development

The Foundation works to strengthen rural livelihoods, encourage local entrepreneurship, promote sustainable agricultural practices, protect natural resources and create opportunities for women, youth, farmers and marginalized communities.

We believe that villages should become centres of economic opportunity, environmental sustainability, cultural preservation and social harmony.

Our work is guided by the principle that development must reach every individual and every community.

Our philosophy

Our name represents two powerful ideas:

Gramya
Village, rural life and community-based development.
Sarvodaya
The welfare, upliftment and prosperity of all.

Together, Gramya Sarvodaya represents the holistic development of rural communities for the welfare of society.

Inspired by Gandhian ideology

Our philosophy draws inspiration from Mahatma Gandhi's principles:

Sarvodaya
Welfare of All.
Gram Swaraj
Self-reliant and self-governing villages.
Swadeshi
Promotion of local products and industries.
Ahimsa
Peace, harmony and non-violence.
Satya
Truth and transparency.
Trusteeship
Responsible use of resources for society.
Antyodaya
Priority to the most disadvantaged.

We believe that strong villages create a strong nation.

Our vision

To build an inclusive, sustainable and self-reliant rural society where every individual has access to livelihood opportunities, education, healthcare awareness, economic participation and a dignified life.

Our mission

  1. Promote sustainable agriculture and natural farming.
  2. Strengthen farmer institutions and collective enterprises.
  3. Empower women and rural youth through skills and entrepreneurship.
  4. Establish rural industries using local resources.
  5. Promote environmental conservation and renewable energy.
  6. Encourage green education and environmental awareness.
  7. Develop agro-tourism and rural livelihood opportunities.
  8. Promote community participation and financial inclusion.
  9. Support waste management and circular economy initiatives.
  10. Preserve traditional knowledge, culture and rural heritage.
